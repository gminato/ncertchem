# Cite NCERT, never reproduce it

The site mirrors NCERT's Unit and Section structure and numbering so Learners can move between the book and the site. All Prose, figures and questions are original, though. NCERT textbook content is copyrighted by NCERT, and reproducing it on a public site carries legal risk. Writing our own Prose is also where the site adds value. Do not "save time" by pasting textbook passages or redrawing NCERT figures one-to-one.
