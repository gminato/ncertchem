# NCERTCHEM Glossary

Canonical vocabulary for NCERTCHEM, a visual-first learning site for Class 11 and 12 chemistry.

## Audience

**Learner**:
A Class 11 or 12 student preparing for CBSE or state board exams who is studying NCERT chemistry alongside this site.
_Avoid_: User, aspirant

**Teacher**:
A secondary audience who projects Animations and Simulations in a classroom.

## Content Structure

**Unit**:
A top-level division that maps one-to-one to an NCERT textbook unit (e.g. Class 11, Unit 2 "Structure of Atom").
_Avoid_: Chapter

**Section**:
A numbered subdivision of a Unit that mirrors the NCERT section number (e.g. 2.4 "Bohr's Model for Hydrogen Atom").
_Avoid_: Topic, lesson

**Syllabus Map**:
The complete list of Units for both classes, shown to Learners whether or not a Unit is live yet.

**Hook**:
The opening question or puzzling observation that starts every Section.

**Key Ideas**:
The 3–5 point revision summary that closes every Section.

**NCERT reference**:
The NCERT class, unit and section number that a piece of site content corresponds to. The site cites NCERT and never reproduces NCERT text.

**Prose**:
Original explanatory text written for this site that frames and connects Animations and Simulations within a Section.

## Access & Monetization

**Account**:
The signed-in identity that Progress and paid access belong to. A Learner can use every Free Section without one.
_Avoid_: User, profile, login

**Free Section**:
A Section that anyone can open in full without signing in.
_Avoid_: Sample, demo, preview

**Paid Section**:
A Section that opens in full only with an active Subscription. Free and Paid are chosen by hand, Section by Section, with about 30–40% of Sections Free.
_Avoid_: Premium, locked, pro

**Subscription**:
A 365-day fixed-term entitlement tied to an Account that unlocks every Paid Section across all Units in Class 11 and Class 12.
_Avoid_: Plan, tier, membership, recurring subscription

**Paywall**:
The barrier rendered on a Paid Section directly below the Hook when visited by an Account without an active Subscription, prompting the Learner to subscribe.
_Avoid_: Gate, lock screen, blocker

**Lock**:
The visual indicator shown beside a Paid Section on the Syllabus Map and Unit navigation to signal that paid access is required.

**Entitlement**:
The server-verified database record confirming an Account holds an active Subscription with an unexpired validity date.

## Progress

**Progress**:
A Learner's own record of the Checks they have answered correctly; wrong attempts and revealed answers are never recorded.
_Avoid_: User progress, score, marks, grade, tracking

**Complete Section**:
A Section in which the Learner has correctly answered every Check it currently holds.
_Avoid_: Finished, done, mastered

## Visual Content & Interaction

**Animation**:
A guided, linear visual that the Learner plays or scrubs through where the author controls narrative and the Learner controls playback.
_Avoid_: Video, GIF

**Simulation**:
An interactive model where the Learner changes inputs (sliders, toggles, drag) and sees the system respond.
_Avoid_: Interactive, demo, widget

**Flagship Simulation**:
One of the few high-investment Simulations in a Unit, chosen because the concept is best understood by experimenting with it.

**Scrolly Animation**:
An Animation pinned beside the Prose, where scrolling through the Prose advances it.
_Avoid_: Scrollytelling

**Step**:
One discrete state of an Animation, tied to a single passage of Prose. Animations advance Step by Step.
_Avoid_: Frame, slide, scene

**Check**:
A short question embedded in a Section that lets the Learner confirm understanding. Never graded.
_Avoid_: Quiz, test, exercise
